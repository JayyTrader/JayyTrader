Built for long-term valuation research, not high-frequency signals.

Scoring Direction

The approved SDCA scoring rules determine how each raw indicator maps to its individual score. The intended composite direction is +3 for cheap and −3 for expensive Bitcoin.

Daily Update

The planned automation runs at 00:00 UTC. Each feed keeps its own source timestamp because not every provider publishes new data every day.

Data Integrity

Missing, stale, or invalid readings must be flagged. The system must not invent readings, silently replace failures with neutral scores, or fabricate historical observations.
