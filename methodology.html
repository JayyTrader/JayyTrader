Research / Protocol

Methodology & sources

JxProbability publishes transparent Bitcoin valuation research and systematic accumulation intelligence. The public interface and operator research system must use the same genuine observations and calculations.

Data integrity rules

  • No fabricated market readings or historical observations.
  • Every observation must retain its genuine source and observation date.
  • Source methodology and attribution remain attached to the data.
  • No score transformation or interpretation is assumed without explicit approval.
  • Missing, delayed, invalid, and stale data must remain distinguishable.
  • The live Coinbase BTC/USD quote is separate from valuation calculations.
  • MVRV-Z Score is the first planned indicator; its pipeline remains unavailable until implemented and verified.
  • Additional indicator calculations require separate review.
